# Data sources — Lunar Landing Site Navigator (Member 1: Data Engineering)
# Generated 2026-11-14.
# Terrain comes from LOLA DEM products; site coordinates come from NASA NSSDCA / LROC tables.

================================================================================
TERRAIN — LOLA digital elevation models (DEMs)
================================================================================

1. LDEM_80S_20MPP_ADJ  —  LOLA 20 m/pixel south polar DEM (80S to pole)
   URL:  https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/LOLA_20mpp/LDEM_80S_20MPP_ADJ.TIF  (~2.6 GB GeoTIFF/COG)
   Page: https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/90
   Use:  PRIMARY high-resolution terrain. Read windowed (never load the full grid).

2. LDEM_80S_40MPP_ADJ  —  LOLA 40 m/pixel south polar DEM (80S to pole)
   URL:  https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/LOLA_20mpp/LDEM_80S_40MPP_ADJ.TIF  (~685 MB GeoTIFF/COG)
   Page: https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/90
   Use:  Development + reduced-resolution fallback for the online tier.

3. LDEM_80S_80M  —  LOLA 80 m/pixel south polar DEM
   URL:  https://imbrium.mit.edu/DATA/LOLA_GDR/POLAR/IMG/LDEM_80S_80M.IMG  (PDS3, 111 MB)
         https://imbrium.mit.edu/DATA/LOLA_GDR/POLAR/JP2/LDEM_80S_80M.JP2  (JPEG2000)
   List: https://imbrium.mit.edu/BROWSE/LOLA_GDR/POLAR/SOUTH_POLE
   Use:  Online tier for custom coordinates. NOTE: PDS3/JP2 are NOT readable by
         rasterio's bundled GDAL (GTiff only) — deferred until a full GDAL build is added.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTIONAL — terrain quality / visualization (not downloaded yet)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. LDEM_80S_20MPP_ADJ_ERR / _EFFRES / _HILL  —  20 m error, effective-resolution, hillshade
   Page: https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/90
   Use:  Data-quality indicator (ERR) and optional map hillshade (HILL).

5. 5 m/pixel site DEMs  (per-site products)
   Page: https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/78
   Use:  Optional P1 high-res for specific sites. ~90% interpolated pixels — do not
         claim real 5 m accuracy.

6. LDEC count map + error layers
   Use:  Optional data-quality indicator for the DEMs.

================================================================================
SITE COORDINATES
================================================================================

7.  NASA NSSDCA — Apollo landing sites
    URL: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/lunar/lunar_sites.html
    Use: Apollo site coordinates (Wagner et al., Icarus 283, 2017; Lunar Module coords).

8.  NASA NSSDCA — lunar artifact impacts (landers / rovers / impactors)
    URL: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/lunar/lunar_artifact_impacts.html
    Use: Coordinates for all non-Apollo landed/impacted hardware in sites.yaml.

9.  LROC / Arizona State University site pages
    URL: per-site (linked from the NSSDCA tables); https://www.lroc.asu.edu
    Use: Cross-check individual sites and pull updated imagery/coordinates.

10. LROC 2016 table
    Use: Elevation values recorded in sites.yaml (reference surface not stated — verify).

11. NASA IM-1 page
    Use: Elevation value for IM-1 (reference surface not stated — verify).

12. NASA RELEASE22-089 — Artemis III candidate landing regions
    URL: https://www.nasa.gov/press-release/nasa-identifies-candidate-regions-for-landing-next-americans-on-moon
    Use: Names of the 13 candidate regions. NOTE: the release has NO coordinates; the
         lat/lon in sites.yaml are approximate [Verify] placeholders.

13. LROC QuickMap — Artemis III candidate-region layer
    URL: https://quickmap.lroc.asu.edu
    Use: Verify the Artemis III region polygons/coordinates before promoting a candidate.
