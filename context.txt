## KEY contract details (for a future session)
- DemTile.data = float32, meters above 1,737,400 m sphere, NaN = nodata.
- DemTile.x0 / y0 = projected coords of the UPPER-LEFT pixel CENTER (not corner).
  read_geotiff shifts rasterio's corner origin by +dx/2 (x) and -dx/2 (y).
- South polar stereographic, lat_0=-90, lon_0=0, R=1,737,400 m. Planetocentric
  lat (south negative), east-positive lon in [-180,180].
- select_tile(lat, lon, max_range_km, obs_h_m=2.0) returns a square window of
  2*max_range_km on a side, centered on the site (windowed read, never full
  load). Falls back to next tier if a read fails (e.g. incomplete 20 m).
- coverage_check returns covered=False + "COORD_OUT_OF_COVERAGE" for lat > -80,
  truncated=True when dist_from_pole + range > ~304 km coverage radius.

## Key findings / decisions (also in docs/decisions.md)
1. Bundled GDAL has ONLY the GTiff driver — no PDS3, no JPEG2000. 80 m IMG
   deferred; 40 m COG is the reduced-res fallback.
2. CRS is IAU 2015 ("Moon 2015 Sphere / Ocentric"), NOT MOON_ME (DE421).
   Projection math identical, but ~0.029 deg (~875 m) frame offset. We keep
   frame="MOON_ME" for the contract, log a WARNING, and flag for Member 2.
3. 40 m COG already height-in-meters (no conversion). Unit detector confirmed.
4. COGs are EVEN grids (15200x15200): pole at a pixel CORNER, not a center
   pixel. Validator uses center=(n-1)/2 with +/-0.5 tolerance.
5. PGDA TLS is flaky: curl exit 56 needs --retry-all-errors (plain --retry
   won't retry it).
6. Downsampling default = average (np.nanmean); --method max available.

## Horizontal error budget (asked by user)
- DEM sampling: +-10 m (20 m) / +-20 m (40 m) half-pixel.
- Frame offset (IAU 2015 vs MOON_ME, uncorrected): ~875 m systematic.
